SOURCE NOTES

Sources &
methods.

304 original practice questions cover all 135 numbered subsections of the 2025 ORNAC 17th edition. Each answer links to your Drive copy and identifies relevant PDF pages alongside the printed page labels.

Coverage

The bank contains 75 safety, 69 infection prevention, 60 perioperative phases and anesthesia, 39 ethical and professional, 37 exceptional clinical events, and 24 managing resources questions. Sixty questions form 15 four-part cases.

Mixed exams use midpoint weights within the CNA perioperative exam blueprint ranges: 12.5%, 25%, 22.5%, 20%, 12.5%, and 7.5% respectively. These are study targets, not a prediction of any individual exam. CNA describes approximately 165 items with 15–25% in case sets.

Cases are grouped when available in a mixed exam. Domain-filtered or missed-question sessions focus on the selected pool.

Study method

The answer order is shuffled. The first answer reveals correctness, a short rationale, an explanation of the other choices, and concept basics. Missed and saved questions use this browser's local storage. The 17th-edition bank starts with fresh progress; earlier bank progress is not included. No account or patient data is collected.
